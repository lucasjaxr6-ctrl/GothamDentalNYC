Digital design and in-office milling replace the two-visit routine and the temporary crown.

The Wand® single-tooth anesthesia

A computer-guided, pen-like handpiece that numbs only the tooth being treated. Pressure, not pain.

Questions

Before your first visit.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. Book online any time or call us during office hours. At your first visit we take digital X-rays, review your history and build a personal treatment plan. You can fill in your forms online beforehand.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

You are not alone. We offer The Wand for gentle, localized anesthesia, sedation options, color-therapy glasses and a team that explains every step before it happens.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call (212) 253-1850 right away. We offer same-day emergency appointments whenever possible.

How often should I come in?

Every six months for a cleaning and check-up, as the ADA recommends. Regular visits catch decay and gum disease early.

Is professional whitening safe?

Yes. We use medical-grade agents adjusted to your sensitivity level for strong results with minimal discomfort.
